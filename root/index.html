Sleep & Recovery Analytics — V2 Beta

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

Sleep & Recovery Analytics (“the Application”) is operated by Shanmukh Jonnalagadda. This notice describes the multi-user beta. Each invited user connects their own WHOOP account and receives a separate personal dashboard.

Information accessed

Google sign-in supplies identity claims such as a stable account identifier, verified email address and token expiry. The email is checked against the invitation list and displayed during the session. A pseudonymous identifier derived from Google's issuer and account identifier keys stored account records.

With your WHOOP authorization, the Application accesses your WHOOP member ID, recovery, physiological cycles and strain, sleep and workouts. WHOOP's profile response can include your name and email; this implementation retains the member ID and discards the other profile fields. The Application does not receive or store your Google or WHOOP password.

OAuth access and refresh tokens are retained in encrypted form to synchronize your data when you are not actively using the Application. Personal targets and synchronization status are also stored. Optional journal CSV uploads are processed in the current browser session and are not deliberately persisted to the database or repository.

Uses

Information is used to authenticate invited users, connect the correct WHOOP member, synchronize personal records, calculate analytics, display comparisons and observations, generate requested reports, remember targets and operate or troubleshoot the Application. Data is not sold or used for advertising.

Storage and service providers

Streamlit Community Cloud hosts and processes the application; Supabase stores account records, encrypted WHOOP tokens, targets and health metrics. GitHub hosts private source code and runs scheduled synchronization through GitHub Actions. Google provides login and WHOOP provides authorized wearable data. Necessary credentials are supplied through hosting and workflow secret settings.

These services may retain operational logs or backups according to their own settings and policies. Application logs are designed to omit tokens and personal health records. Downloaded reports are stored wherever you save them.

Access and sharing

Ordinary users can access only their own account's stored dashboard data. The operator administers the hosting and database systems and can access stored records for operation, support and deletion requests. Infrastructure providers process data as necessary to supply their services. Information may also be disclosed when legally required. WHOOP and Google process information under their own policies.

Retention, disconnect and deletion

Data is retained while you use the Application unless you delete it or request deletion. Disconnect WHOOP removes stored tokens and stops future synchronization but retains your saved history and targets. Delete my app data removes the active account record, stored metrics, targets, tokens and pending authorization state.

The Application attempts to revoke WHOOP access during these actions. If remote revocation cannot be confirmed, remove the integration in WHOOP settings as well. Deletion from the active database does not instantly erase provider backups, existing downloads or information previously displayed in an open browser tab; provider backups follow the configured service retention policies. Signing in and reconnecting after deletion creates a new account record.

You may request access to or deletion of your stored information using the contact below. Requests will be handled within 30 days unless retention is legally required. Removing an email from the invitation list alone does not disconnect an existing WHOOP integration.

Choices and security

Participation is optional. You may stop using the Application, decline authorization, disconnect or revoke access, delete app data and contact the operator about your information. Server-side access controls, encrypted WHOOP tokens and protected secret settings are used to safeguard information. No online system can guarantee complete security.

Health information and age

The Application provides informational and educational analytics, not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. It is not intended for children under 18.

Changes and contact
