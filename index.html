Sleep & Recovery Analytics

A personal analytics dashboard for WHOOP users.

Sign in with Google, connect your own WHOOP account, and view your personal sleep, recovery, strain, and workout trends in one dashboard.

What the application does

Sleep & Recovery Analytics helps invited users review their own WHOOP data through personal trend charts, recovery briefings, sleep analysis, strain and workout views, and downloadable summaries.

Each user signs in with Google and connects their own WHOOP account. User data is separated by account so each invited person receives an individual dashboard.

Data and privacy

The application uses Google sign-in for authentication and WHOOP authorization to retrieve data the user explicitly permits. The application does not receive or store Google or WHOOP passwords.

For details about information access, storage, synchronization, deletion, and service providers, read the Privacy Policy.

Important note

This application provides informational and educational analytics only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
