# Privacy Policy for Personal Sleep & Recovery Analytics

**Effective date:** September 7, 2026

Personal Sleep & Recovery Analytics (“the Application”) is a personal analytics application operated by Shanmukh Jonnalagadda. This policy explains how information is accessed, used, stored, and deleted when you connect your WHOOP account.

## Information We Access

With your permission through WHOOP’s OAuth authorization process, the Application may access:

* Recovery information, including recovery score, HRV, and resting heart rate
* Physiological-cycle information, including strain
* Sleep information, including sleep duration and performance
* Workout information, including activity type and workout strain

The Application does not receive or store your WHOOP password.

## How Information Is Used

Your information is used only to:

* Synchronize your authorized WHOOP data
* Generate personal sleep, recovery, strain, and workout analytics
* Display trends, comparisons, and personalized observations
* Generate reports requested by you
* Operate, maintain, and troubleshoot the Application

The Application does not use WHOOP data for advertising or sell personal information.

## Data Storage and Service Providers

Authorized data and OAuth tokens may be stored or processed only as necessary to operate the Application. The Application may use third-party infrastructure providers, including Streamlit Community Cloud and GitHub, for application hosting and private project storage.

Credentials, access tokens, and personal health information will not intentionally be published in a public repository.

## Data Sharing

Personal information is not sold or rented. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the Application or disclosed when legally required.

WHOOP separately processes information according to its own terms and privacy practices.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only while necessary to provide the Application’s functionality or complete development testing.

You may disconnect the integration through your WHOOP account or request deletion of stored information by contacting the email address below. Deletion requests will be handled within 30 days unless retention is legally required.

## Your Choices

You may:

* Choose whether to authorize the Application
* Decline any requested WHOOP permission
* Revoke the Application’s WHOOP access
* Request access to or deletion of stored information
* Stop using the Application at any time

## Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect information. However, no online storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Health Disclaimer

The Application provides personal analytics for informational and educational purposes. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

## Children’s Privacy

The Application is not intended for children under 18 years of age.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the Application develops. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
