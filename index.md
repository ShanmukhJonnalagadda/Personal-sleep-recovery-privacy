# Privacy Policy for Personal Sleep & Recovery Analytics

**Effective date:** September 7, 2026

Personal Sleep & Recovery Analytics (“the Application”) is a personal health and fitness analytics application operated by Shanmukh Jonnalagadda. This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, used, stored, shared, protected, and deleted when you sign in and connect your WHOOP account.

## Information We Access

### Google Sign-In Information

When you sign in using Google, the Application may receive basic account information such as:

* Your name
* Your email address
* Your Google account identifier
* Your profile picture, if available
* Your email-verification and authentication status

This information is used only to authenticate you and determine whether your email address is approved to access the Application.

The Application does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Contacts, Google Calendar, or other Google services. It does not receive or store your Google password.

### WHOOP Information

With your permission through WHOOP’s OAuth authorization process, the Application may access:

* Recovery information, including recovery score, HRV and resting heart rate
* Physiological-cycle information, including strain
* Sleep information, including sleep duration and sleep performance
* Workout information, including activity type and workout strain
* Information required to synchronize and process these records
* OAuth access and refresh tokens required to maintain the authorized WHOOP connection
* Synchronization status and timestamps

The Application does not receive or store your WHOOP password.

### Technical Information

The Application and its infrastructure providers may automatically process limited technical information necessary to provide and secure the service, such as:

* Browser and device information
* Internet Protocol address
* Authentication and session information
* Request times
* Application errors and operational logs

## How Information Is Used

Information is used only to:

* Authenticate your identity through Google
* Restrict dashboard access to approved email addresses
* Maintain and secure your login session
* Synchronize your authorized WHOOP data
* Generate personal sleep, recovery, strain and workout analytics
* Display trends, comparisons and personalized observations
* Generate reports requested by you
* Maintain synchronization status
* Diagnose synchronization or application errors
* Operate, secure, maintain and improve the Application

Google account information is not used to access other Google services.

The Application does not use personal information or WHOOP data for advertising, marketing profiling or automated decisions with legal effects. Personal information is not sold or rented.

The Application’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including its Limited Use requirements.

## Cookies and Authentication Sessions

The Application uses Streamlit’s authentication system and Google OpenID Connect to manage login.

After you sign in, Streamlit stores an identity cookie in your browser. This cookie allows the Application to recognize your authenticated session and may remain for up to 30 days unless you log out or remove it sooner.

Logging out removes the Application’s identity cookie for the relevant session. Logging out of the Application does not necessarily sign you out of your Google account or close other Application sessions that were already open.

Google and the Application’s infrastructure providers may use their own essential cookies according to their respective privacy practices.

## Data Storage and Service Providers

The Application uses the following third-party services:

* **Google Identity Services** authenticates users and provides permitted basic account information.
* **WHOOP** provides the health and fitness information authorized by the user.
* **Streamlit Community Cloud** hosts the Application and supports authentication sessions.
* **Supabase** stores processed WHOOP records, synchronization information and encrypted WHOOP OAuth tokens.
* **GitHub** provides private source-code storage, protected application secrets and scheduled synchronization through GitHub Actions.

WHOOP OAuth tokens are encrypted before being stored in Supabase. Application credentials and service secrets are stored using the secret-management features supplied by Streamlit and GitHub.

Credentials, OAuth tokens and personal health information are not intentionally committed to or published through a public source-code repository. GitHub Actions may temporarily process credentials and synchronization requests while performing scheduled operations.

Google account profile information is used for authentication and access control and is not intentionally added to the WHOOP analytics database.

Each service provider may process limited information according to its own terms and privacy policy:

* [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* [WHOOP Privacy Policy](https://www.whoop.com/us/en/full-privacy-policy/)
* [Streamlit Privacy Policy](https://streamlit.io/privacy-policy)
* [Supabase Privacy Policy](https://supabase.com/privacy)
* [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

## Data Sharing

Personal information is not sold or rented.

Information may be processed by the service providers identified above only as necessary to:

* Authenticate users
* Host and operate the Application
* Retrieve authorized WHOOP information
* Securely store and display analytics
* Perform scheduled synchronization
* Diagnose errors
* Protect the Application and its users

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, regulation, legal process or a valid governmental request.

## Data Retention and Deletion

WHOOP information, synchronization records and encrypted OAuth tokens are retained only while necessary to provide the Application’s functionality, maintain the authorized connection or complete operational testing.

Revoking the Application’s WHOOP access prevents future access to your WHOOP account but may not automatically delete information that was previously stored. You may request deletion of stored information using the contact information below.

Deletion requests will be handled within 30 days unless continued retention is legally required. Some limited information may temporarily remain in provider backups, security logs or operational records according to the applicable provider’s retention practices.

Authentication cookies are retained according to the session behavior described above. Google may separately retain authentication information according to its own privacy policy.

## Your Choices

You may:

* Choose whether to sign in using Google
* Choose whether to authorize access to your WHOOP information
* Decline any requested WHOOP permission
* Log out of the Application
* Revoke the Application’s WHOOP access
* Request access to or deletion of stored information
* Stop using the Application at any time

Google Sign-In is required to access the protected dashboard.

## Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect information. These safeguards include:

* Restricting dashboard access to approved email addresses
* Verifying authentication through Google
* Encrypting WHOOP OAuth tokens before storage
* Keeping credentials in configured secret-management systems
* Using secure connections supplied by the infrastructure providers
* Limiting information access to what is necessary to operate the Application
* Continuing to display previously saved data when WHOOP is temporarily unavailable

However, no method of online transmission, processing or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
